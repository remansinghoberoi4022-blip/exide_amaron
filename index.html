Signs your battery may need attention

These are common things drivers notice. If you see them, talk to us.

Slow engine start

The engine turns over slower than usual.

Dimming lights

Headlights or dashboard lights look weaker.

Repeated starting issues

You need several attempts to start.

Warning indicator

A battery light appears on the dashboard.

Long periods unused

The vehicle has been parked for a long time.

Hard starts in some conditions

Starting is harder at certain times or in certain weather.
